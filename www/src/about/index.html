About

Software with one name on it.

mimux is built and maintained by Matteo Merola — an engineer in Zurich who got tired of choosing between webmail that spies and mail clients that can't be automated. It's independent software: no investors, no growth targets, no reason to ever hold your mail hostage.

What it's built on

The free/pro line is published, and it doesn't move.

Everything a human does in mimux is free, AGPL-3.0, forever. Everything a machine does through mimux — the API, the MCP server, webhooks — is paid. That line was written down publicly before the paid layer existed, precisely so it can be held against us later.

Verifiable beats trustworthy.

"No telemetry" is a claim; make verify-free is a proof. The free build's dependency graph is checked in CI to contain zero commercial code, licence verification runs offline, and every licensing claim on this site has a corresponding check in the repository.

Boring technology, long horizons.

One Go binary, one SQLite file, htmx on the front. Chosen not because they're fashionable but because they'll still compile, restore and run in ten years. Email is decades old; a mail client should plan to be.

Small by design.

mimux is single-admin on purpose. It will not grow teams, tenants, or an enterprise tier — it grows in depth, not width. If you need multi-tenant email infrastructure, excellent products exist; this isn't trying to become one.

The company

mimux is a product of Merola Software & Services, the independent software business of Matteo Merola, based in Zurich, Switzerland. The client is open source under AGPL-3.0; the automation layer is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0; the whole arrangement is documented in LICENSING.md.
